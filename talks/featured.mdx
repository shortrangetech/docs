These hand-picked talks offer the best introduction to OpenDoorsim, its design philosophy, and its use in accessibility and UX research. Whether you are new to the project or looking to deepen your understanding, these presentations are an excellent place to start.

## Must-Watch Presentations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Building Open Hardware for Accessibility" icon="universal-access">
    A deep dive into how OpenDoorsim was designed with accessibility testing in mind. Covers sensor selection and compliance frameworks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="OpenDoorsim v0.2 Demo" icon="play">
    Live demonstration of the OpenDoorsim rig and companion app. Great for newcomers to understand the full system.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Door Simulation for UX Research" icon="magnifying-glass">
    How HCI researchers use OpenDoorsim data to study user interaction patterns with physical interfaces.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
