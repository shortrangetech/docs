title: "LAB Case Build Guide"

It's time to enclose your OpenDoorSim Board and accessories in an awesome case!

<Steps>
  <Step title="STEP ONE: MAGNETS" icon="magnet" titleSize="h2">
    <Note>
      **Official kits come with the decal tile already assembled.**
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      Avoid snapping magnets together as they may break from impact.
    </Warning>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Install Decal Tile Magnet" titleSize="h3">
        Insert 1x thin magnet (20x10x3) into the decal tile.

        **<u>North</u>** side should face in and towards the logo.

        Once inserted, only the **<u>south</u>** face of the magnet should be visible.

      <Step title="Install Decal Tile Mount Magnet" titleSize="h3">
        Insert 1x thin magnet (20x10x3) into the case body for the decal tile mount.

        **<u>North</u>** side of the magnet should face out and away from the case. This magnet serves to snap the decal tile to the case.

        Once inserted, the decal tile should snap to the case!

        Insert 4x thin magnets (20x10x3) into the end caps.

        Only the **<u>south</u>** side of the magnet should be visible after insertion.

        After insertion, test that the end caps snap to the case!

        <Tip>
          If magnets do not stay seated in the end caps, use some industrial superglue.

  <Step title="STEP TWO: THREADED INSERTS" icon="fire" titleSize="h2">
    <Warning>
      Plastic gives off harmful fumes when heated or burned. Work in a well ventilated area and avoid direct inhalation.
    </Warning>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Case Body - Align Threaded Inserts" titleSize="h3">
        Align 13x threaded inserts over the insert holes. The small end of the insert should set nicely in the insert hole.

        <Frame>
          ![IMG 6037](/images/IMG_6037.jpeg "IMG 6037")
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Case Body - Heat Press Threaded Inserts" titleSize="h3">
        Once inserts are aligned, heat press with a soldering iron or threaded insert press. **Inserts should end up flush or slightly under the body line**.

  <Step title="STEP THREE: COMPONENTS" titleSize="h2">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Install OLED Screen" titleSize="h3">
        Mount the OLED screen using 2x short screws (M3x4) on the **<u>outermost mounting holes, leaving the innermost mounting holes clear</u>** to allow for other components.

        <Warning>
          Mounting the OLED using the two innermost holes will make it so your other components do not sit flush. If you wish to use those threads, obtaining a flatter or lower profile screw would allow for sufficient clearance. We have found mounting the screen with the outermost holes to be sufficient.
        </Warning>

        Align the pogo wire with the mounting hole.

          Be careful not to press directly on the magnets of the pogo wires or you may push them out.

      <Step title="(Optional) Run Pogo Wires to GPIO pins">
        If desired, route the pogo wires to "X" and spare power screw terminals on the ODS board (X1, X2, X3, X4, X5, X6, etc). These are currently open in the hardware / software design for future module implementation.

        <Note>
          There are no official modules (yet), and as such, there is no official wiring for module data and power pogo wires. Wire and implement at your own risk!
        </Note>
      </Step>
