title: "LAB Case Build Guide"
---

It's time to enclose your OpenDoorSim Board and accessories in an awesome case! 

<Steps>
  <Step title="Insert 1x Decal Tile Magnet" titleSize="h2">
    Insert 1 thin magnet (20x10x3) into the decal tile with the **<u>NORTH</u> **side facing in towards the logo.

    Once inserted, only the **<u>SOUTH</u>** face of the magnet should be visible.

    <Note>
      Official kits come with the decal tile already assembled.
    </Note>
