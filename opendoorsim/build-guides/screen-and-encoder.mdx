# Build Guide

<Card title="Wiring Warning" type="warning">
  Ensure you are wiring power and data correctly between your accessories and the ODS board.  Because wire harnesses come in many different colors (even in the official kits!), it is unwise to completely rely on the colors you see in the photos below for correct routing.

  Use the board, accessories, and wires you have on hand for reference — only you can make sure you are routing them correctly.
</Card>

        <Tip>
          TIP: Plugging the JST connector into the board will allow you to confidently route / solder your wire to the screen. Make sure the four wires are connecting as indicated in the table below:
        </Tip>

        | ODS BOARD | wire | SCREEN |
        | --- | --- | --- |
        | GND | ←→ | GND |
        | \+3V | ←→ | VDD / VCC / POWER |
        | SCL | ←→ | SCL |
        | SDA | ←→ | SDA |
