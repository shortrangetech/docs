## Sourcing Tips

For structural materials, local hardware chains such as Home Depot or Lowe's are the most convenient and cost-effective option — lumber and door panels are bulky to ship. For electronics, reputable online suppliers including [Adafruit](https://www.adafruit.com), [SparkFun](https://www.sparkfun.com), and [Digi-Key](https://www.digikey.com) carry all the sensors, microcontrollers, and wiring accessories listed above. General-purpose items like breadboards, jumper wires, and USB cables can often be sourced more cheaply in bundles through Amazon.

<Tip>
  Order 10–20% extra on small consumables like sensors, jumper wires, and reed switches. These components are easy to damage during soldering or testing, and having spares on hand avoids delays waiting for a replacement shipment.
</Tip>
